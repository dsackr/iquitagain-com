<p style="padding-left: 30px;"><em>This is an unfinished Draft from 02/11/2009 - I'm not sure why I didn't published back then... </em></p>
Those two words drive me crazy!  I hate saying them.  Its like wearing the proverbial red A on my chest (from the <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Scarlet-Letter-Bantam-Classics/dp/0553210092/ref=nosim/sackriderorg-20">Scarlett Letter</a>) telling the whole world - Yes I have tried and failed before - but,
<blockquote>
<h2>"This Time..."</h2>
</blockquote>
I hate those words.  I mean it!  Everytime I talk to someone about losing weight, or this blog, or anything health related - I have to say those two words.  I named my blog "I Quit... Again!" because of those two words.  I have quit and quit and quit every time I've tried before - thus the name, "I Quit... Again!" as an ironic twist to those two words - "This time".

I've tried to lose weight before - many times, but "<strong>This time</strong> I started at 308lbs" and "<strong>This time</strong> will be different" and "<strong>This time</strong> I have a better support group" and "<strong>This time</strong> I have cooler electronics to help me".
