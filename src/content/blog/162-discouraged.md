<p>Yesterday I ran Pat's Run. Last weekend I ran 18 miles. This month I have run more than any other month this year so far and I still have two weeks left in the month.</p>
<p>I have logged my food this month too. I would say more constantly than ever before. Most days I've kept my calorie count under 2300 but never under 1700.</p>
<p>In every way I know how to measure this has ben a GREAT month, except... I have GAINED weight!</p>
<p>This is where my friends usually say something like, but you have most likely gained muscle. Thanks but that isn't encouraging...</p>
<p>I'm discouraged and my eating habits this weekend show it...</p>
