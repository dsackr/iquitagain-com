I've often said my first goal in weight loss was to lose the "morbid" descriptor from my obese title. Using a standard BMI calculator, I've accomplished this goal. At 5'9" and 249 lbs, my BMI is ~37. Morbid obesity is defined at above 40 BMI.

BUT - I'm not a fan of BMI. I prefer a body fat percentage test like that one I've gotten from the BodPod in the past. Its been a while and I think the lowest I ever weighed when I was doing those test regularly was somewhere in the low 280's.

I'll see if I can find the company I used to do the BodPod testing through before and keep you posted...
