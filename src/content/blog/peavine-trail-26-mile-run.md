<p>It took me 7 hours and 40+ mins to "run" 26 miles... Not a great time but the elevation gain was over 1200ft and the trail is at 5100ft and it was over 85 degrees when I finished... </p>
<p>On race day (in just 2 more weeks!) it will be less than a few hundred ft gain, less than 3200ft above sea level, and less than 75 degrees temp... </p>
<p>Still over an hour  and twenty mins above race day pace doesn't inspire confidence...</p>
