Easy day - I did go for a short two mile run with a one and a half mile walk.  I still have no idea what my Feb races are going to be!

While one the run today, I had a conversation with my brother-in-law about being overweight.  I realized that when I buy a 2x shirt - I'm no longer trying to find clothes that fit - I'm looking for clothes I can fit in.  The difference might seem subtle but its there.  Soon I hope to be buying clothes that fit...
