Calling all weight loss bloggers!!!  When I started this blog a couple of years ago I came across the <a href="http://biggestloserblogedition.blogspot.com/">Biggest Loser Blog Edition (BLBE)</a>, a weight loss competition among weight loss bloggers themed around the popular NBC show, the Biggest Loser.

Sadly, I didn't make it through the full <a href="https://iquitagain.com/weightlossjournal/biggest-loser-blog-edition-my-wi">16 weigh ins</a>.  I did however meet (virtually) a lot of great people, several had already lost significant amounts of weight, while others were still looking to get started.  Several of the links I had to their blogs are dead, the sites are gone, the bloggers off the grid.

I know <a href="http://angiealltheway.blogspot.com/">AngieAllTheWay</a> (as far as I know, the creator of the BLBE) is still going strong since her blog shows that she recently had a child and is thinking of starting back on Weight Watchers with their new <a href="https://iquitagain.com/weightlossjournal/weight-watchers-propoints">ProPoint</a> system.  But what about <a href="http://mundrusmom.blogspot.com/">Just Jen</a>, <a href="http://hotchpotchery.blogspot.com/">HotchPotchery</a>, or <a href="http://acakeforawife.blogspot.com/">A Cake for a Wife</a>?

I don't remember if I met Ronie through BLBE or not, but she is still blogging away at <a href="http://ronisweigh.com/">Ronie's Weigh</a> - and even has a popular vlog.  It appears that she has also started a weight loss blogging community at <a href="http://www.blogtolose.com">Blog to Lose</a> - with over 4000 members currently - WOW!

Recently <a href="http://twitter.com/GymMama">@GymMama</a> has been encouraging me in my running efforts on twitter - I'm not sure if she has a weight loss effort underway or not, but she does <a href="http://confessionsofagymmama.blogspot.com">blog about life</a>.

According to the last post on BLBE (May 14, 2009):
<blockquote><em>This was a long challenge and it was very hard to keep up with, so if and when there is another one, it won't be until after a much needed break</em></blockquote>
This is the part where I usually boo and hiss, but I understand how difficult it can be to keep 'real life' going and blog - so adding a weight loss blogging competition to the mix must have been difficult.  If BLBE never rises again, I'm still grateful to Angie for hosting it and all the people I met along the way that encouraged me as I got started on this crazy IQA ride!

If you are a weight loss blogger and happened by - even if you have never been a part of the BLBE - I would love to hear from you!  Leave me a comment or send me an <a href="mailto:dale@sackrider.org">email</a>!
