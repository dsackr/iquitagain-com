<h2>wk12.03.25.2012 – 252 lbs</h2>
One pound... I should be happy. Today marks the end of 12 weeks and in 12 weeks, I've lost 36 pounds. That's pretty exciting... but

I am so anxious to get into the 240's. I knew losing 5 lbs in a single week without Biggest Loser style workouts would be impossible - but I had hopes. I only needed 4 lbs to get into the 240's but 5 lbs would have put me at 40 lbs down for the year.

Truth is - if I continue to lose a pound a week for the rest of the year, I would be 212 lbs by Christmas!
