I'm guessing you are already starting to think about what you will resolve to accomplish next year in 2010.  I have the added pleasure of being able to read through several past years thoughts... I'm not sure what my new New Year's Resolutions will be, but most of them will be the same old New Years Resolutions.  You could almost take those old posts and just change the year... I still need to lose weight, I'm still working on that degree, I'm still paying off debt, I'm still trying to find a savings plan that works for me, I'm still trying to read more books... yada yada yada...

I'll have a clever post soon for 2010 - maybe "10 goals for 2010" or "20/10 - 20lbs in 10 weeks"... I like that one...
