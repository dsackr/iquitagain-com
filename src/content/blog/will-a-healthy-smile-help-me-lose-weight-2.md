<p>You have heard the phase "putting the cart before the horse", right?</p>
<p>I'm not sure the link between how you feel about yourself and how much you weight is that simple.  When you feel like a slob, you treat yourself that way. You dress poorly, do you hair poorly, eat poorly, ect.</p>
<p>Included in that list, at least for me, is taking care of my teeth. Don't get me wrong, I brush and even floss. But going to the dentist just hasn't been on my list.  Today I'm at the dentist. I have several issue, none of which are causing me pain. </p>
<p>I've never been happy with my smile, but never did anything about it for a lot of reasons, including money. But being here in the dentist chair (yes, I'm in the chair as I write this) makes me think about why I haven't fixed my teeth... Why I haven't fixed my smile.</p>
<p>Similarly, I've often said if I were thinner, I would be able to dress better.  But what if I have it backwards? Would I be thinner if I dressed better?</p>
<p>Would a healthy smile, a better wardrobe, a better self image, make me more likely to stay with a diet, more likely to stick with an exercise program, etc?</p>
<p>I think it could... What do you think?</p>
