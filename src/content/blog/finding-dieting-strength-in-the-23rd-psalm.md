<p align="left"><span style="font-family: 'Wedding Text'; font-size: x-large;">T</span><span style="font-size: large;">he Lord is my Shepherd; I shall not want. [<span style="color: #ff0000;">cake, donuts, or pizza</span>]
He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: [<span style="color: #ff0000;">and eat green veggies</span>]
He leadeth me beside the still waters. [<span style="color: #ff0000;">no soda or juice</span>]
He restoreth my soul:
He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for His name' sake.</span></p>
<p align="left"><span style="font-size: large;">Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, [<span style="color: #ff0000;">due to morbid obesity</span>]
I will fear no evil: For thou art with me;
Thy rod and thy staff, they comfort me. [<span style="color: #ff0000;">as well as your carrots and celery</span>]
Thou preparest a table before me [<span style="color: #ff0000;">of low sodium soups and high nutrient salads</span>] in the presence of mine enemies; [<span style="color: #ff0000;">fast food restaurants</span>]
Thou annointest my head with oil; [<span style="color: #ff0000;">but put no oil in my food</span>] My cup runneth over. [<span style="color: #ff0000;">again, with just water</span>]</span></p>
<p align="left"><span style="font-size: large;">Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life,
and I will dwell in the House of the Lord forever.</span></p>
