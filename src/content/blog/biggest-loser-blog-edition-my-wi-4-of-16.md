Drum Roll Please... After a long week of temptations with both successes and failures, my weigh in this morning was...

288lbs - THE SAME AS LAST WEEK!

I'm glad there wasn't a gain, I would have rather had a loss of course... I'm still very excited about breaking 285lbs, and I feel like this will be the week.  I'm reminded everyday of the 285lbs number; I have post-its at my desk, my mirror in the bathroom, my laptop wallpaper, and anywhere I can put it.  I burned over 5,000 calories two days in a row (both Saturday and Sunday) and my goal is to break 4k each day this week.  I have been wearing the bodybugg arm band for a full week and I know that I currently burn about 3,500 calories a day, so 4k cals a day is a stretch goal for me (and in line with my "<a href="/weightloss-articles/a-month-to-live">A Month to LIVE</a>" challenge)!

Sorry I wasn't able to post a loss for Team Lean and the Biggest Loser Blog Edition Weight Loss Challenge - I promise I'm not working as a double agent for the A-Team (Team Angie) - I will push on and I will lose weight!
