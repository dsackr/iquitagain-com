<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">Top ten things I hate to hear from people after starting a new effort to lose weight:</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">10: Oh, you’re eating healthy again?</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">Yeah – I’m dieting again. Yes, that means I’m going to say no to your offer of junk food. YES – I failed last time. YES – I’m likely to fail THIS TIME but I really don’t need that tone, the one that says “should I hold this junk food until next week when you will be off the diet?”</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">9: It’s not a diet; it’s a life style change.</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">Getting a promotion at work that doubles my income would be a life style change. Eating salad every day and telling myself I’m never going to eat six slices of pizza in a single meal again is depressing and if I’m more than two days in, I’m already sick of hearing that it’s not a diet.</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">8: You look great!</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">I know you mean this to encourage me but unless I’ve actually lost a significant amount of weight, it RARELY feels like a genuine statement. Especially if I just started my “life style change” last week. Instead, it just comes across like “thank God, you are finally doing something about your health – I hope you don’t quit this time”.</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">7: You should drink more water</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">You know – I think you are right – maybe I’m over weight because I’m dehydrated! </span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">6: Anything about water weight</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">I don’t know what water weight is. I know the human body is like 80% water. So couldn’t I lose my entire weigh loss goal in water? Usually I assume the reason people don’t notice when I lose the first 15-20lbs, it’s because I’m just that over weight – not because it’s all “water”…</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">5: Maybe you gained muscle and that’s why you stopped losing weight</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">More likely, I started eating junk again and I’m actually gaining fat – or maybe I’m drinking too much water and its all water weight. But I appreciate the sediment.</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">4: You’re still lapping everyone on the couch!</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">I don’t know why this one bothers me. People that say it are only trying to be encouraging. But I just want to reply with “not on a treadmill – I’m just kinda running in place, not lapping anyone.”</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">3: You know what works for me is…</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">I know people are just trying to relate in some meaningful way when they start sharing their weight management efforts with me, but what works for you isn’t very likely relevant to me. That is, unless you were 100lbs over weight and a food addict… if that’s the case, then by all means, please do share what worked for you.</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">2: I’m so proud of you!</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">Unless you are my wife or parent – why would this motivate me? Are you saying that, not only should I seek your approval, but until I started a weight loss effort, I didn’t have it?</span></span>
<h3><strong><span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">1: Iceberg lettuce has no nutritional value</span></span></strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"><span style="font-family: Calibri;">SO WHAT! I’m already struggling with my choices in life – I’m fat and starting a diet (um, sorry, I mean life style change), and when I finally make a decent choice to have a salad – that’s not good enough, I have to eat kale and bok choy before I get your nod of approval? I hope you choke on a chicken bone. Not to death, just long enough that you need my help to perform the Heimlich maneuver, and I’m going to brag afterward that my iceberg salad gave me the strength to save your life.</span></span>
