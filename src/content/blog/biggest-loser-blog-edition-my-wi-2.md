<p style="padding-left: 30px;">Starting weight: 288
Last week WI: 285
This week WI: 289

Being true to myself and my blog - I have updated MyFitnessPal.com with my new weight and the beautiful "23lbs lost" now read "19lbs lost".  But, on the bright side - it does still read "lost" and while this week on the road, I may have lost focus, I haven't lost sight of my goal.  Tomorrow is my last traveling day and then its back to pounding the pavement with Nike+ and watching the food intake.  Sorry Team Lynn... I'll make it up next week!
