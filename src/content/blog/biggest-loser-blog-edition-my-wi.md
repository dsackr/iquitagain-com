Sorry for the post being so short guys, but I'm out of town.  I didn't want to leave anyone hanging for too long regarding my BLBE WI (Weight In).  I took my Nintendo Wii and Wii Fit with me on the trip and plugged it into hotel TVs along the way for the read trip - which became a great motivator to stay ON the diet while driving 2000 miles.  My WI day is Saturday, and I was worried that I would put on weight due to the trip.  I actually did very well - I lost 2 lbs since the inital checkin almost two weeks ago.

I'm not on track for my 3 lbs a week goal but considering that I had a huge 6 1/2 lb GAIN last weekend (<a href="https://iquitagain.com/weightlossjournal/there-a-snake-in-my-garden-salad" target="_self">Read about that here</a>)- this WI was one for the win column.  To be honest, 3lbs a week is a very agressive goal - and if I only lose one a week, I'll still be 52lbs lighter at the end of the year than I started - AND THAT WOULD BE AWESOME!
