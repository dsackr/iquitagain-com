I've always wanted to jump out of a plane but I'm too heavy at my current weight of 275lbs (not counting the pounds I likely put on today due to my <a title="Cheat Day – a really bad idea" href="https://iquitagain.com/weightlossjournal/cheat-day-a-really-bad-idea">cheat day</a>.

This isn't a new goal but I've failed every time before this - to maybe <a title="This Time" href="https://iquitagain.com/weightlossjournal/this-time">this time</a> will be different! Ha! We both wouldn't bet on it, but maybe since you are reading this post, you are as hopeful as I am...
