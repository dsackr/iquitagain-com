I have no idea if I will ever do this or not - but here's the idea... I want to redesign the website into three sections:

Food: This is where I would put all my dieting posts, ideas, and failures. I would talk about what diet plan I'm trying, what food I'm eating, calories in, etc.

Exercise: The activities I have planned, the daily grinding runs, training, calories out, etc.

Technology: The shoes, the GPS watch, the apps, the pedometers, etc.

I'm thinking like a three column home page where you could enter each section. I don't know if it will help - but I like the idea. Do you have any thoughts on it?
