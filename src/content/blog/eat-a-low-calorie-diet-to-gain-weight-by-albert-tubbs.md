<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small; ">Ever eat food you hated because it was low cal and still not keep weight off? I use to all the time.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">The program with all those programs for me was twofold:</span>
<ol>
	<li><span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small; ">White-knuckle all the time.</span></li>
	<li><span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small; ">Coming off the program.</span></li>
</ol>
<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">The white-knuckle was a zone that I would enter into in order to follow the diet program. Eat this and don’t eat that. I could be in this zone for long periods of time but always knowing I could not live in this zone for the rest of my life.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">This always led to the problem of coming off the program and eventual weight gain, AGAIN.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Paul McKenna has changed my total perspective of food. Okay, maybe it is more like changing it not changed.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">I still have trouble looking at all the food on my plate and saying “I’m full, leave it”.  That’s not my biggest problem though. I have noticed that TV watching and over eating went hand and for me and that has been hard to break.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Who knew you could say no to so much food by just asking yourself “am I full”?</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Today I did eat more but I don’t believe it was in a rage or crazy craving. I do have to vigorously fight one urge, weighing myself. I’ll explain more about this one tomorrow.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Breakfast:</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Cooked 2 eggs, 1 potato, ½  red onion, and ¾ a red pepper. Ate all the veggies nut a little egg and ¼ of the potato.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Lunch:</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Fiber bar and yogurt. Ate it all.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Dinner:</span>

<span style="font-family: 'Times New Roman'; font-size: small;">Salad &amp; pudding. Didn’t eat all the salad. Later had 2 spoonfuls of peanuts with some chocolate chips and then 3 slices of ham (for the record I don’t think I ate any of this on a binge or emotional craving).</span>
