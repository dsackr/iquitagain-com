<p>Today I decided that I'm going to stop sabotaging my weight loss efforts.</p>
<p>I've been discouraged and just as in the past, I've let that send me hurling down the wrong path.</p>
<p>Last weekend I pretended that I don't care about my health and I ate like I believed my own lie.</p>
<p>But today I am looking for strength. I want to be a runner. I want to be healthy. And I want to be thin!</p>
