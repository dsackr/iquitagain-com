<p>Today I woke up with renewed faith in my ability to stick with this weight loss effort. I have been very discouraged lately, specifically the last week or so. But Monday, I got back on the wagon and started logging my food again (I did not log any food saturday or sunday).</p>
<p>The scale at one point over the weekend read 283 lbs!  But today I am back in down to 270, and I think this weekend I could see something around 268!  </p>
<p>OK... feet back on the ground... There is still a lot of work left to do...</p>
