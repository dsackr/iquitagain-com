Last night I blogged about being in a <a href="https://iquitagain.com/a-year-on-purpose/291-im-in-a-dark-place">Dark Place</a> - the place where I start to sabotage my own weight loss efforts but today while surfing my old posts, I came across <a href="https://iquitagain.com/weightloss-articles/book-reviews/what-sex-shaving-and-bad-underwear-have-to-do-with-weightloss">a quote from Dan Ariely</a> describing the place where I find myself
<blockquote><em>...you decide that you’re done acting a certain way (No more smoking! No more spending! No more unprotected sex!), but then when temptation strikes, you go back on your word</em></blockquote>
The quote came directly from Dan Ariely's blog post titled "<a href="http://danariely.com/2009/11/15/sex-shaving-and-bad-underwear/">Sex, Shaving, and Bad Underwear!</a>" where Dan suggests that in order to follow through with our goals (weight loss, exercise, saving money, whatever), we may need to setup <em>pre-commitments</em> that force us down the right road.

When I closed my post last night with "I’m going to climb out of this place", I was referring to this feeling I have that there is a light at the end of this tunnel - but it wasn't until I re-read Dan's post that I realized what that feeling was.

I have already signed up for a 12k race on Saturday.  I have plans to run the half marathon in January.  I may be running a marathon rely with my brother-in-law in March, and I have a set date to run the full marathon with my brother-in-law September of next year.

I've setup quite a few pre-commitments (aka my Bad Underwear - read Dan's post) that are keeping me focused.  I'm going to continue running.  I'm going to continue losing - yes, both weight and races! :D

My point here - I'm NOT Quiting!
