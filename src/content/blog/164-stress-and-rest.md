<p>Today is a day I have to live on purpose. I know everyday is supposed to be a day on purpose, but today I must be careful to do nothing... And do that intentionally.</p>
<p>Yesterday I got a new PR for my 5k time but I wasn't happy because I was losing time with each mile. My ego says to me, "you could have done better, get out there this morning and prove it!"</p>
<p>But my head knows that anything I do today would be what Galloway calls junk miles. My legs need time to heal from yesterday. Stress the legs, then rest the legs.</p>
<p>So today, my goal is to do nothing... Like Bruno Marrs' The Lazy Song... Nothing at all!</p>
