Just a quick note - this morning I ran my normal 5k run, but I was working on speed - I finished with a 5k time of 33:16 - a full 12 seconds off the prior 5k PR of 33:28.

I'm sure its ego - but I felt like I should have been a little faster still.  I have Pat's Run on Saturday - so I'm not running tomorrow at all.

After Saturday - I'm looking to include a speed training day once a week.  I'm concerned that if I don't get faster, I'll have an issue with completing the marathon in the time I have available.

I haven't had a PR in a long time - so for now I'm going to celebrate the victory...
