I'm not trying to insult you... but everyone is <strong>FAT</strong>, including YOU!  You may think you are just a little over weight - but you are wrong - you are fat.  I don't mean 'fluffy' or big boned, either.  What is 'big boned' anyway;<em><strong> you have never seen a fat skeleton</strong></em>!  It may not be politically correct to say "FAT" but <strong>overweight</strong>, heavyset, <strong>chunky</strong>, fluffy, <strong>big boned</strong>, on the heavy side, <strong>thick</strong>, large, <strong>horizontally challenged</strong>, lovable,  <strong>plump</strong>... however you want to say it - <em>we are all there</em>.  If you think I'm wrong, check your ideal weight here - <a href="http://www.healthstatus.com/calculate/iwc" target="_blank">http://www.healthstatus.com</a>.  Put in your sex and height, it will tell you what you should weigh.  I thought I needed to lose 100lbs.  I was wrong.  I need to lose 150 lbs!  That's right, my ideal weight is 152 lbs.  That's One Five Two.

At first, I thought "No way! Anything under 200 is just too small for a guy with my build."  But who am I kidding?  We have adjusted our perception of overweight in America.  We think that an extra 10 or 20 lbs is ok - and maybe it is... but we have redefined 'extra'.  It should mean anything above our ideal weight - but if you ask anybody these days, NOBODY expects to be down to their 'ideal' weight.  Believe it or not, I was poised to write an article railing against the idea that someone can tell me what my ideal weight is based on my height alone.  I was wrong.

I played football in high school and although even then I was <span style="text-decoration: line-through;">overweight</span> <strong>FAT</strong>, I did have a lot of muscle (in my opinion).  I weighed 220lbs and I thought if I could just get down to 200lbs, I would be prefect.  There are a lot of online articles out there telling you that your ideal weight cannot be based on height alone, and I'm sure that people a lot more educated in these matters than me could argue either way for hours - but I have made up my mind.  My ideal weight is 152lbs, and my goal of 195lbs just <em><strong>isn't going to cut it</strong></em>!

I am a food-a-holic, and I am tackling step four of the twelve step program - "Made a searching and fearless moral inventory of ourselves".  I am no longer afraid to admit that I am MORBIDLY OBESE.  I know that I need to lose almost 150lbs to be healthy.  That is my fearless inventory.  So stop saying to yourself, "I need to lose a few pounds" and take a look at how much you really are overweight.  Are you willing to take a fearless inventory of yourself?
